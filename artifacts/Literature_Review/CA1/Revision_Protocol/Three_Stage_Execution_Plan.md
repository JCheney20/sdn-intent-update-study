1. Keep the independent raw-input oracle separate from the numeric candidate adapter. Extend correspondence to actual joint old/new packet executions; compare packet edges, boundary states and final acknowledged tables, not only PASS labels.
2. Materialize the bounded one-new-request source slot. Preserve request identity/arrival through waiting and admission; explicitly report a second outstanding request as outside the bounded domain. Seed pending-loss, arrival-reset, hidden authorization/destination-based gate and false-completion defects.
3. Exercise controller progress and blocked results, action slots, acknowledgement-based drainage and the complete removed-edge history lifecycle, including the final retained boundary and first expired boundary. No probe location or delivery may guide controller choices.
4. Add ordinary, CCG-inspired, guard-only, detector-guided and guarded-detector lifecycle adapters, preserving their supported semantics. Name adaptations precisely; no claim of reproducing the full published CCG implementation or its in-switch buffering. Do not silently turn every method into Q or grant an uncharged V fallback.
5. Independently validate each generated controller trace, including adverse intermediate states, complete safe coverage, unsafe witnesses, capacity refusals and blocked/incomplete results. A failed method can be a valid finding; a checker mismatch is a stop-and-repair issue.

**Gate:** all included adapters have explicit domain/progress arguments, complete independent agreement on the frozen tiny cases and successful required defect controls. Until then no new primary result is accepted.

## Stage 3 — freeze and execute sensitivity, matched costs and revised primary comparison

The author has authorized execution of this stage. The following is the prospective design, not results. Final machine-readable manifests and source hashes must be frozen before the corresponding stage-3 outcomes are observed; changes require a dated rationale, not favorable-outcome selection.

- **Workloads:** original changing-intent Cisco artifact (10 forwarding nodes, 12 hosts) and fat tree k=4 (20 switches, 16 hosts). No invented eight-switch reproduction, larger topology or extra traffic contract. The sealed constructed continuous-service study stays a separately labelled historical control.
- **Rows:** baseline per-switch maximum of old/target untagged occupancy, plus extra-row allowances 0 and 1. The latter is derived from the already specified V construction, not an outcome-selected threshold. Gates, tag assignment and source slot storage are separately counted.
- **Methods:** ordinary, Q, V, declared CCG-inspired scheduler, guard-only, detector-guided and guarded-detector. Freeze each fallback and detector component boundary before outcomes. Unsupported/blocked methods remain visible.
- **Detector conditions:** k=3, k=20 and the existing explicitly labelled k=3-plus-adjacent-host adaptation. Prove actual helper arguments, especially the artifact Cisco CLI's hardcoded k=20. Full host-pair snapshot alarms are not restricted to the temporal model's legitimate requests.
- **Native-RNG sensitivity:** 32 prospectively fixed seed labels 20261101 through 20261132, separately by topology/configuration/language. Seed labels are not common random numbers. Report cache/decision/qualification changes and exhaustive-baseline alarm coverage. This is a descriptive bounded sensitivity study, not a powered population-equivalence claim; no unapproved ±5-percentage-point equivalence assertion.
- **Primary:** separate fixed seed labels 20261025, 20261026 and 20261027 for detector-dependent rows. Seed-independent controls execute once per topology/row allowance, not as duplicate independent workloads per cache configuration. Retain all failures, partials and unattempted cells. Freeze one attempt per primary cell; an interrupted primary is not automatically retried or split to renew its allowance.
- **Costs:** separately report cache setup/augmentation, decision/guard/scheduler work, final independent checking, applied control batches/row mutations and peak rows. Cold startup/JIT is separate from warm work. No logical-round to real-latency conversion.
- **Matched benchmarks:** only overlapping snapshot detector/sampling/replay components have a Python comparator. Five cold process invocations per condition; one declared warm-up followed by 20 timed repetitions per condition in alternating Python/Julia order. Use equal resources and validated outputs. Sparse/neighbor sampling is a named adaptation/optimization condition, not pure language speedup. If a repetition exceeds 120 seconds, report incomplete and pause that condition rather than silently lengthening it. Final manifests must identify exact timed calls, stopping policy and measurement boundaries before execution.

**Gate:** Stage 1 and Stage 2 pass; source/environment/trace manifests and measurement/grid/attempt policy are frozen and verified. Then execute the authorized comparison, report qualification before costs, retain negative/inconclusive findings, and synthesize manuscript-ready tables with the claim boundaries intact.
